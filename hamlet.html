STAGE ARCHIVE / 1991

HAMLET

A theatrical record preserved through performance photographs, presented with the quiet rhythm of an archival exhibition.

The Performance

Eleven restored black-and-white photographs are kept together as one continuous theatrical sequence. Each image is presented at the same visual scale, allowing the production itself, rather than the layout, to carry the drama.

UNDER THE DIRECTION OF GHOTBEDDIN SADEGHI

The production was guided by Ghotbeddin Sadeghi, whose direction brought together the ensemble, visual composition, and dramatic structure of the performance.
